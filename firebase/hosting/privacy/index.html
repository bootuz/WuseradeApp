Wuserade Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Wuserade (Усэрадэ) is an iOS app for reading Kabardian poetry. It is offered by its developer, “the Wuserade developer” (“we”). This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. You can read poems without signing in.

What we collect

  • An account ID. When you first open the app, it quietly creates an anonymous Firebase account for you, which is just a random user ID. We use it so your liked poems can be saved. No name or email is needed for this.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). If you choose to sign in, your Apple or Google account is linked to that same account, so your likes stay. If you already have a Wuserade account with that Apple or Google account (for example from another device), you are signed in to it instead: your likes move into it and the anonymous account is deleted. Firebase Authentication then receives your account identifier and the email address and name that Apple or Google shares with us. Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your real one.
  • Your likes. For each poem you like, we store the poem’s ID, the time you liked it, and a copy of the poem’s title, author name and text. Your likes are saved under your account in Cloud Firestore (a Google Firebase service), in the europe-west3 (Frankfurt) region.
  • Photos you send. Only readers signed in with Apple or Google can send a photo for a poet. The app removes the photo’s location and camera data on your device before it is uploaded. The photo is then stored in Firebase Storage (a Google Firebase service) in the United States (us-central1), linked to your account, until we review it. If we approve it, it is published on the poet’s page and is not linked to you. If we reject it, it is deleted. A waiting photo is also deleted when we approve a different photo of the same poet. If you delete your account, photos of yours that are still waiting are deleted; approved photos stay on the poet’s page. A blocked account cannot send photos.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics). Which screens you view and what you do in the app, for example which poem you open, what you search for and what you like. Firebase Analytics also collects some details on its own: your device model, operating system version, app version and language, an app instance ID (a random ID for this copy of the app), and your approximate location (country or city), which Google works out from your IP address. This is tied to your Firebase user ID.
  • A push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). A code that identifies your device so we can send notifications. The app creates this token when it starts, even if you decline notifications. Your choice only controls whether notifications are shown to you.

Your display settings (such as font and dark mode) stay on your device and are not sent to us.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the app: to keep your likes, to let you sign in on another device, to send notifications, and to understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • The app has no ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who handles your data

The app runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Analytics and Cloud Messaging). Google processes your data on our behalf. Your likes are stored in Frankfurt, and photos waiting for review are stored in the United States; other Firebase services may process data in other countries. See Firebase privacy and security for how Google handles it.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in with Apple or Google: open Settings in the app and tap Delete account. This deletes your account, all your likes, your profile and any photos you sent that are still waiting for review, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Approved photos stay on the poet’s page.
  • Using the app without signing in: your likes are saved under a random ID that is not linked to your name or email. Deleting the app does not remove them from our servers. To delete them, sign in with Apple or Google (your likes move to that account), then go to Settings and tap Delete account, which deletes your account and all your likes.
  • Usage data: analytics data is kept and handled according to Google Firebase’s data retention. You can email us to request deletion of the analytics data tied to your ID.

Children

Wuserade has a section of poems for children. The app creates the same anonymous ID and usage data for every user, including children. No account is needed to read. A parent or guardian can email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, data linked to a child’s use of the app.

Your rights and contact
