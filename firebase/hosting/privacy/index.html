Wuserade Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Wuserade (Усэрадэ) is an iOS app for reading Kabardian poetry. It is offered by its developer, “the Wuserade developer” (“we”). This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. You can read poems without signing in.

What we collect

  • An account ID. When you first open the app, it quietly creates an anonymous Firebase account for you, which is just a random user ID. We use it so your liked poems can be saved. No name or email is needed for this.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google (optional). If you choose to sign in, it links to that same account. Firebase Authentication then receives your account identifier and the email address and name that Apple or Google shares with us. Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your real one.
  • Your likes. For each poem you like, we store the poem’s ID, the time you liked it, and a copy of the poem’s title, author name and text. This is saved under your account in Cloud Firestore (a Google Firebase service), in the europe-west3 (Frankfurt) region.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics). Which screens you view and what you do in the app, for example which poem you open, what you search for and what you like. This is tied to your Firebase user ID.
  • A push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging). A code that identifies your device so we can send notifications. The app asks your permission before it shows notifications.

Your display settings (such as font and dark mode) stay on your device and are not sent to us.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the app: to keep your likes, to let you sign in on another device, to send notifications, and to understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • The app has no ads.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who handles your data

The app runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and Cloud Messaging). Google processes your data on our behalf. Your likes are stored in Frankfurt; other Firebase services may process data in other countries. See Firebase privacy and security for how Google handles it.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in with Apple or Google: open Settings in the app and tap Delete account. This deletes your account, all your likes and your profile, and revokes Sign in with Apple.
  • Not signed in: there is nothing to delete in the app. You can sign out or delete the app. To have the likes saved under your anonymous ID removed from our servers, email us.
  • Usage data: analytics data is kept and handled according to Google Firebase’s data retention. You can email us to request deletion of the analytics data tied to your ID.

Children

Wuserade has a children’s poetry section, but the app is not designed to collect data from children. No account is needed to read, and the anonymous ID and usage data described above are created the same way for every user. A parent or guardian can email us to ask for deletion of data linked to a child’s use of the app.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing astemirboziy@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something new, we will update this page and the date at the top.
